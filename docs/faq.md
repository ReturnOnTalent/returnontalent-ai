# Frequently asked questions

## Is ReturnOnTalent AI available today?

The core engine has been delivered in partnership with AI Singapore; the product is not yet released. WeLinkTalent is now productising it — authentication, interface and evaluation — and opening a small number of pilots.

## Who built the retention engine?

WeLinkTalent co-developed the core engine with AI Singapore's AI Apprenticeship Programme (AIAP), Industry track, Batch 22. The intellectual property belongs to WeLinkTalent Pte Ltd.

## Who is it for?

CHROs, Heads of People Analytics and HR transformation leads, and the CFOs and COOs who hold the budget for retention.

## Does it replace our HRMS?

No. It sits alongside your HRMS — Workday, SuccessFactors and others — and adds the retention layer those systems do not cover.

## Is employee data safe?

ReturnOnTalent is designed to comply with PDPA and GDPR. HR governs the process, and every score carries its source evidence.

## What does "no score without proof" mean?

Every score cites the field and the evidence behind it. A figure can be challenged and traced to its source — the difference between a dashboard and a decision.

## What does "no score without a version" mean?

Every output carries the model, the prompt version, the weights and the date behind it. Results stay auditable, run after run.
