# Roadmap

## Live today

- **Internal Mobility** process — running in production today.
- **Retention pool** — running in production today.

These are the foundation the AI modules are built on. They work today on the ReturnOnTalent platform.

## Launching Q1 2027

- **Attrition Prediction** — a score per employee, with risk bands and the reasons behind the score.
- **Hidden Talent Identification** — a view of performance and potential mapped to career intent.

## Then

- **Sentiment Analysis** (stand-alone) — a sentiment index with the evidence behind every score.
- **Executive Report** — one consolidated, auditable summary uniting attrition, mobility and sentiment.
- **Enterprise controls** — single sign-on, multi-tenant and usage-based billing.

## Built with AI Singapore

The core engine was co-developed with AI Singapore's AI Apprenticeship Programme (AIAP), Industry track, Batch 22, under the French Chamber of Commerce in Singapore AI Lab framework. The intellectual property belongs to WeLinkTalent Pte Ltd.

## Pilot partnerships

We are opening a small number of pilot partnerships for companies that want retention decided on evidence. [Request early access](https://www.returnontalent.io/contact-us).
